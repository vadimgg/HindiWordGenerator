RULES

1. Do not output `hindi`, `romanisation`, `english`, `title`, `subtitle`,
   `source_ref`, `audio`, or output filenames. Rust copies those from trusted
   planner/source data.
2. Use the input row `id` exactly as given.
3. `register` must be one of: `informal`, `standard`, `formal`.
4. `tokens` are visible word occurrences in sentence order. Do not include
   spaces, punctuation, commas, dashes, question marks, danda/full stop signs,
   or symbols.
5. `words` are explanation entries within the card. Every token must reference
   exactly one `words[]` entry by `word_id`, and every `words[]` entry must be
   referenced by at least one token.
6. If the same visible word appears twice, include two tokens. They may share
   one `words[]` explanation entry when the meaning is the same.
7. `tokens[].roman` must reconstruct the input `romanisation` when tokens are
   joined with the spaces and punctuation from the input `romanisation` string.
8. Use tilde nasalisation only: `maĩ`, `yahā̃`, `haĩ`, `gharõ`. Do not use
   `ṃ`, `ṁ`, or `o̐`.
9. `literal` should show Hindi word order, even when it sounds unnatural.
10. Word `meaning` and `note` values must use simple English, not grammar terms.
11. Optional word fields such as `gender`, `number`, and `note` may be omitted
    when not useful. Do not use null or empty strings.
12. Do not invent uncertain information.
